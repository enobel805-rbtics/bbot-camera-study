Blockage checks

The released 2026 field mesh was registered against all 32 WPILib tag centers (maximum residual 0.966 mm). Nine points on each tag were ray-tested against the actual triangles; any obstruction excludes the whole tag. Opaque-only and all-surfaces results agreed. A positive-control ray through a tower upright was detected; an outside-field control ray stayed clear. The current drivetrain export and an earlier shooter export did not block the tested rays. Tower upright parts GE-26502-01 / GE-26502-02 did.

Practical conclusion

The upward tilt is usable at the two exact shooting poses. Changing tilt alone cannot move a line of sight around a tower upright: camera position and robot position determine that blockage. Robust two-tag coverage needs a position change or complementary camera coverage, followed by another check.

This checks simulated visibility, not physical Limelight decoding or pose accuracy. Camera calibration, illumination, exposure, motion blur, wiring, flex, and moving mechanism states are not validated. One visible tag can provide a measurement but does not establish adequate pose quality for auto driving.
